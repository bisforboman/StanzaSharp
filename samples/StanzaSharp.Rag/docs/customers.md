# Customer notes

## Kessler Chemicals

Kessler Chemicals ships solvents from Hamburg to Lyon every week. Their buyer, Paul Marchetti, asked for temperature
logs on every load after a delivery arrived warm in July. Ingrid Solberg agreed to fit loggers to the trailers used on
that route.

## Nordlys Seafood

Nordlys Seafood moves frozen fish from Bergen to Rotterdam. The contract renews in January. Their logistics manager,
Sigrid Aune, wants the new Rotterdam warehouse to include a cold store, which the plan does not have yet. Tomas Venn is
pricing it.
