# Who does what

Halvard Freight has its head office in Gothenburg and depots in Antwerp and Rotterdam.

## Operations

Ingrid Solberg leads operations. She joined from the Port of Gothenburg in 2019 and runs the depots, the drivers and
the new warehouse project.

## Finance

Tomas Venn is the finance director. Budgets for new buildings go through him before the board sees them.

## Sales

Amira Haddad runs sales from the Antwerp office. She looks after the chemical customers, including Kessler Chemicals,
and visits Lyon twice a year.
