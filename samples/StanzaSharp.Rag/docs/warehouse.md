# The Rotterdam warehouse

## Decision

The board of Halvard Freight approved the new warehouse in Rotterdam on 12 March. It replaces the two rented halls near
the old harbour, which the company leaves at the end of next year. Ingrid Solberg, the head of operations, presented the
plan together with Tomas Venn from finance.

## Responsibilities

Ingrid Solberg is responsible for the building and the move. Tomas Venn keeps the budget and reports to the board every
quarter. The architects are Brandt & Okafor, a small office in Utrecht that designed the company's depot in Antwerp.

## Timeline

Construction starts in May. The first trucks should load at the new docks in the autumn of the following year. Until
then, the halls near the harbour stay open, and nothing changes for customers.
