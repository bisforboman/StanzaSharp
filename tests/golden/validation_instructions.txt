Lemon Ricotta Pancakes
Serves 4. Prep time: 10 min. Cook time: 20 min.

Ingredients:
• 1 ½ cups all-purpose flour
• 2 tbsp sugar
• 1 tsp baking powder
• ½ tsp baking soda
• ¼ tsp salt
• 1 cup ricotta cheese
• ¾ cup milk
• 2 large eggs, separated
• zest of 1 lemon
• 2 tbsp. butter, melted, plus more for the pan

Directions
1) Whisk the dry ingredients together in a medium bowl.
2) In a second bowl, combine the ricotta, milk, egg yolks, lemon zest and melted butter.
3) Beat the egg whites until stiff peaks form, about 2–3 minutes.
4) Fold everything together gently. Don't overmix!
5) Heat a skillet over medium-low heat. Cook ¼-cup portions for 2 min per side.

Tip: if the batter seems too thick, add another tablespoon or two of milk. Leftovers keep in the fridge for up to 3 days.
