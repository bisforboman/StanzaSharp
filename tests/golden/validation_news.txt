HARBORVIEW, Ore. — The Harborview Port Authority approved a $214 million expansion plan on Wednesday, ending a two-year dispute with local fishing cooperatives. The vote was 5-2. Commissioner Dana Whitlock, who cast one of the dissenting votes, called the plan "a gamble with other people's money."

Under the agreement, the authority will dredge the north channel to a depth of 14 m (about 46 ft.) and build two new berths by Q3 2028. Construction is expected to create roughly 1,100 temporary jobs. Critics say that number is inflated.
